# Escalation Contacts

> **EXAMPLE DEFAULTS.** Edit before production rollout.

When a question sits outside this onboarding, direct the learner to the matching contact below.

---

## AI Onboarding and Program Questions

**Who to contact:** AI Program Lead
**Example:** "How do I use OpenCode for this project, and how do I ask for a new approved use?"
**Contact:** ai-program@example.com or #ai-program on Slack.

---

## Internal Policy and Governance Questions

**Who to contact:** AI Program Lead
**Example:** "Is OpenCode approved for this kind of client work?"
**Contact:** ai-program@example.com or #ai-governance on Slack. Pause the task until you have an answer. Write to privacy@example.com when the task includes client data. Write to legal@example.com when the material is a legal document.

---

## Security Concerns

**Who to contact:** Security Team
**Example:** "I think I shared sensitive material with the AI tool."
**Contact:** security@example.com or #security on Slack. Report an active incident the same day. If you are not sure it is a security concern, report it anyway.

---

## Technical Issues

**Who to contact:** IT Helpdesk
**Example:** "OpenCode is not working, or I cannot connect to an approved MCP server."
**Contact:** helpdesk@example.com or #it-helpdesk on Slack.

---

## Data Handling and Privacy

**Who to contact:** Privacy Team
**Example:** "Which data label applies before I paste this into a prompt?"
**Contact:** privacy@example.com or #privacy on Slack.

---

## Legal and Compliance

**Who to contact:** Legal
**Example:** "Legal told us not to put this document in an AI tool. What do I do with the task?"
**Contact:** legal@example.com or #legal on Slack.

---

## Who Owns AI Decisions

**Who to contact:** Executive sponsor
**Example:** "Two teams disagree on whether a use is approved. Who decides?"
**Contact:** executive-sponsor@example.com. The Executive sponsor owns AI decisions, including a policy change and a dispute between teams. The AI Program Lead runs the program each day. For a use question, start with the AI Program Lead at ai-program@example.com or #ai-program on Slack. Reach the Executive sponsor through your manager or through the AI Program Lead.

---

## When You Are Not Sure

**Who to contact:** AI Program Lead
**Example:** "I do not know which team answers this."
**Contact:** ai-program@example.com or #ai-program on Slack. The AI Program Lead routes the question. Do not continue a task you are unsure about while you wait.
